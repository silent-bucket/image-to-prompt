# Shot Analysis Cheatsheet

Visual cue → terminology mappings, used when analyzing a photo in the
`image-to-prompt` skill. Keeps wording consistent run to run.

## Shot size (how much of the subject fills the frame)

| Visual cue | Shot size |
|---|---|
| Only a detail (eye, hand, texture) fills the frame | Extreme close-up |
| Face/head or small object fills most of the frame, little to no background | Close-up |
| Head-and-shoulders to waist-up, some background visible | Medium close-up |
| Waist-up to full figure, background clearly visible | Medium shot |
| Full figure with generous surrounding space | Medium-wide / full shot |
| Subject is small relative to a large environment | Wide shot |
| Scene-setting shot, subject may be absent or tiny | Establishing shot |

## Camera angle

| Visual cue | Angle |
|---|---|
| Camera roughly level with subject's eyes/center | Eye-level |
| Camera below subject, looking up | Low-angle |
| Camera above subject, looking down | High-angle |
| Camera directly overhead | Bird's-eye / top-down |
| Horizon line tilted | Dutch tilt / canted angle |

## Lens / focal length (from perspective cues)

| Visual cue | Likely lens |
|---|---|
| Stretched edges, exaggerated foreground, wide field of view | Wide-angle, ~14–24mm |
| Mild, natural-looking perspective, moderate field of view | Standard, ~35–50mm |
| Flattering compression of facial features, blurred background | Portrait, ~85mm |
| Strong background compression, subject isolated from a distant background | Telephoto, 135mm+ |
| Extremely magnified small detail, very shallow focus plane | Macro |

## Depth of field (from background blur)

| Visual cue | Approx. aperture |
|---|---|
| Background is a smooth wash of color/light, subject sharply isolated | f/1.4–f/2.8 (very shallow) |
| Background softly blurred but shapes still readable | f/4–f/5.6 (moderate) |
| Most of the scene, foreground to background, is in focus | f/8–f/16 (deep) |

## Lighting quality

| Visual cue | Lighting term |
|---|---|
| Crisp, defined shadow edges | Hard light |
| Soft, gradual shadow transitions | Soft light |
| Warm orange/yellow cast | Warm color temperature (~2700–3500K) |
| Neutral white cast | Neutral color temperature (~4500–5500K) |
| Cool blue cast | Cool color temperature (~6500K+) |
| Light source visible in frame, strong directional shadows | Hard direct/backlight or sidelight |
| Even, shadow-minimal lighting | Overcast / diffused / softbox-style |
| Warm, low-angle light with long shadows | Golden hour |
| Cool, low-contrast light, blue tones | Blue hour / dusk |
